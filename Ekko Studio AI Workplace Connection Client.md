**🏢 Ekko Studio AI Workplace** is the complete English-language guide to building an AI-assisted studio workspace. Learn how to structure a shared workspace, connect AI assistants to briefs and docs, automate repetitive studio tasks, and keep human review in the loop — with a clean workspace layout and a rulebook. Built for creative studios, agencies, and product teams that want AI doing the boring parts, not the thinking. **Completely free.** No signup. No paywall. No hidden chapters.
